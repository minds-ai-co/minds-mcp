# Scope and research methods

Minds accelerates the first research pass. It helps teams sharpen a question,
surface objections, compare plausible reactions, and decide which assumptions
deserve validation with real respondents.

It does not replace representative human fieldwork.

## Appropriate uses

- early concept, message, and positioning tests;
- audience and objection exploration;
- segment-comparison hypotheses;
- questionnaire and study-plan iteration;
- screening ideas before commissioning fieldwork;
- structured synthesis of a grounded synthetic panel.

## Uses that require additional validation

- population incidence or prevalence estimates;
- regulated, medical, legal, or safety-critical decisions;
- final claims about representative consumer preference;
- decisions where demographic quotas and sampling error must be measured;
- research that requires direct lived experience or informed consent.

## Method availability

Method support changes over time. Call `list_research_methods` before planning a
study.

- **MaxDiff** executes through a deterministic method adapter.
- **Conjoint** is planned and cannot currently execute.

Do not infer method availability from an old transcript or this static page; the
live server response is authoritative.

## Evidence discipline

Treat synthetic research as decision support:

1. preserve the original research question;
2. record the audience definition and study plan;
3. inspect disagreement, not just the majority answer;
4. identify assumptions that require real-world validation;
5. retain exports alongside the downstream decision.
